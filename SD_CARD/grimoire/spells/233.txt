You spray toxic mist at a creature within range.
Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d12 Poison damage.
@CANTRIP
The damage increases by 1d12 when you reach levels 5 (2d12), 11 (3d12), and 17 (4d12).
